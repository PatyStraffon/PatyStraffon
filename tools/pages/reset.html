Who it's for

Made for where you are

The Reset is the easiest place to begin. It's made for women starting fresh or coming back after six months or more away from exercise.

It's also for women who have been training on their own or following random videos, and want real structure and progress they can see. You choose and adjust your weights, and Paty shows you how to find what's right for you.
