Good to know

Booking & changes

All levels are welcome. Wear comfortable clothes and bring a water bottle. Each event lists its location, schedule and what's included.

The deposit is non-refundable. The balance is refundable up to 30 days before the event. After that, you can transfer your spot to a friend or take a credit for a future event.
