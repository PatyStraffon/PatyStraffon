Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026. Your privacy matters. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you sign up or get in touch: your name and email address, and anything you write to us, for example in the THRIVE mentorship application.

When you buy: your name, email and order details. Card payments are handled by our payment providers; we never see your full card number.

In the programs: the details you share in the Paty Straffon app, such as goals, check-ins and progress, so Paty can coach you.

When you browse: basic technical information, such as your browser and the pages you visit, which our hosting provider uses to keep the site running and secure.

How we count visits

To learn which pages help and where readers lose interest, the site counts visits itself. No cookies, no outside analytics company, no advertising tracker.

What is counted: the pages you open, how far down you read, the parts of a page you reach, how long the page is on your screen, which buttons you click, when you send a form, the site or campaign link that brought you here, and whether you use a phone, tablet or computer.

What is not: your name, email or IP address, and no browser fingerprint. Nothing is sold or shared. These counts are never linked to the email you give us in a form.

How it works: your browser keeps two random numbers, one for this visit and one to recognize a return visit. Clearing your browser's site data removes them. The counts are stored with our hosting provider, Netlify.

Clases virtuales para Fitpass

Cuando entras a las clases virtuales con tu código de Fitpass, te pedimos tu nombre y tu correo. Los usamos para registrar tu acceso a las clases y para saber quién las usa. Solo te enviaremos correos con ideas y novedades si marcas la casilla, y puedes darte de baja en cualquier momento con el enlace de cada correo. Tu navegador recuerda tus datos para que la próxima vez solo escribas el código. Guardamos estos datos en Netlify (formularios del sitio) y, si aceptas recibir correos, en Flodesk. Para ver, corregir o borrar tus datos, o retirar tu consentimiento, escríbenos a paty@patystraffon.com.

In English: when you open the FitPass virtual classes, we ask for your name and email to record your access. We email you only if you tick the box.

How we use it

To deliver what you asked for: the free course, your program, your ebook or your retreat booking. To answer your questions. And, if you choose to join our list, to send you emails about wellness in midlife and our programs. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email.

We never sell your personal information.

Who helps us

We use trusted services to run the business, and they process your information only for that purpose:

  • Netlify: website hosting, sign-up forms and our visit counts
  • Flodesk: our email list and newsletter
  • Trainerize: the Paty Straffon coaching app and program checkout
  • Stripe and Payhip: payments and ebook delivery
  • Amazon: affiliate links in the Shop, which may set cookies when you click them

Some of these services store data outside Canada, for example in the United States.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to emails, at any time. Just write to us.

Keeping it safe

We keep only what we need, for as long as we need it, and use reputable providers with strong security.
