Meet Paty

Movement has been my language for a lifetime

Health coach, menopause coaching specialist, former professional dancer and founder of THRIVE. I help women in midlife build strength, protect their bones and feel at home in their bodies again.

My story

From the stage to strength

I spent my early years as a professional dancer. Later I founded Dance Core, and taught thousands of women to move with joy and confidence.

In midlife, I felt I was doing all the right things, but they weren't working anymore. I had always regulated through cardio and low-carb eating. Then I was diagnosed with osteopenia, and I was losing muscle.

So I went back to school. I became a certified personal trainer, a health coach and a menopause coaching specialist, and I changed my approach. I reversed my osteopenia, gained 4 lbs of muscle, and everything changed. I finally saw clearly that this was not a decline. It was a new chapter, asking me to listen more deeply.

Today that work lives in The M Program, the 21-Day Reset and THRIVE, my somatic cardio class. I'm based in Vancouver, and I coach women wherever they are through my app.

What I believe

Grounded in science, rooted in calm

I

Strength is a practice

Not a punishment. We train to feel capable and strong, never to make up for what we ate.

II

Consistency over perfection

Small, steady habits around sleep, protein and movement add up to real change.

III

Calm is part of the plan

Breath and presence help your body recover. Train with intention, breathe with presence and eat to feel strong.
