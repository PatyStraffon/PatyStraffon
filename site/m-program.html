Why it works

Not a meal plan and a pep talk

Midlife changes the rules. Sleep gets lighter, muscle is harder to keep, energy swings, and the workouts that used to work stop working. The M Program is a calm, science-informed system built around what actually moves the needle now.

No clinical jargon. No fear-based urgency. Just a clear plan you can live with, and a coach who shows you how.

The method

Mind. Metabolism. Movement.

Mind

Calm and consistency

Breath, presence and simple routines that lower stress and help you keep going, even on busy weeks.

Metabolism

Fuel for strength

Protein at every meal, smart carbs and steady blood sugar, so your energy works for you.

Movement

Strength that lasts

Progressive strength and impact training that protects your bones and muscle, in 25 to 35 minutes.

Questions

The details

What if I've never strength-trained before?

You're very welcome. The program meets you where you are, and every move has a gentler option. Paty shows you how to choose and adjust your weights. If you'd like an easier first step, start with the 21-Day Reset.

How much equipment do I need?

A mat, a small Pilates ball, a resistance band and a few light to moderate dumbbells. You'll find Paty's picks in the Shop.

How long are the workouts?

Most take 25 to 35 minutes, so they fit into a busy day.

Is it only for one stage of menopause?

No. The 5 Levers work across perimenopause, menopause and post menopause. The program adapts to where you are.

What happens after the eight weeks?

You can continue with M Momentum to keep your app, your workouts and monthly check-ins going.

Is it safe if I have a health condition or injury?

Most women can join safely. If you have a health condition, an injury or are recovering from surgery, please check with your doctor first, then let Paty know so she can adapt the program for you.
