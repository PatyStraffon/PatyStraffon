The 21-Day Reset

A gentle three-week start to strength, protein and better sleep. For women starting fresh, coming back after time away, or ready to turn random workouts into real structure.

Three weeks

One small step at a time

I

Move

Short strength sessions in the app. Learn the moves and find the weights that are right for you.

II

Nourish

Protein at every meal with simple, satisfying recipes, and steady energy through the day.

III

Restore

A calming evening routine, better sleep and breath, so your body can recover and get stronger.

What's included

Everything you need to begin

  • Daily guided workouts in the Paty Straffon app, 25 to 35 minutes.
  • High-protein recipes that are quick to make.
  • Sleep and recovery habits you can keep.
  • Progress check-ins to see how far you've come.
  • Self-paced: start any day and move at your own rhythm.
Who it's for

Made for where you are

The Reset is the easiest place to begin. It's made for women starting fresh or coming back after six months or more away from exercise.

It's also for women who have been training on their own or following random videos, and want real structure and progress they can see. You choose and adjust your weights, and Paty shows you how to find what's right for you.

Feel-the-difference guarantee

Feel stronger, or your money back

Complete the 21 days. If you don't feel stronger or more energized, email us within 7 days of finishing and we'll give you a full refund.

After the Reset

Ready for more?

Many women continue into The M Program: eight coached weeks with Paty and a small group, covering all five levers in depth.
