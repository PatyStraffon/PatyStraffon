Meet Paty

Movement has been my language for a lifetime

Movement has carried me through every season of my life. I spent my early years as a professional dancer, then founded Dance Core and taught thousands of women to move with joy.

I do this work because midlife is not an ending, and it was never about how we look. It is an invitation to step into our next chapter with strength, and with the tools to feel our best.

Everything I teach comes back to awareness, intuition and self-care. We train with intention, breathe with presence and eat to feel strong, so you feel strong, alert, confident and fully alive.

Questions

The details

Who are your programs for?

Women in midlife, at any fitness level, who want wellness grounded in science and rooted in calm, through peri and post menopause.

I'm a beginner, or I already work out on my own. Is this for me?

Yes, both. The programs are made for women starting fresh or coming back after six months or more away, and for women who have been training on their own or following random videos and want real structure and visible progress. You choose and adjust your weights, and Paty shows you how to find what's right for you. The 21-Day Reset is the easiest place to begin.

How do the programs work?

After you join, you download the Paty Straffon app. Your workouts, recipes and check-ins appear day by day.

What's the difference between the 21-Day Reset and The M Program?

The 21-Day Reset is a gentle, self-paced start in the app: three weeks to build simple habits around movement, protein and sleep. The M Program is the full eight-week method, coached by Paty with a small group, covering all five levers in depth. Many women start with the Reset and continue into The M Program.

How long are the workouts?

Most program workouts take 25 to 35 minutes, so they fit into a busy day. THRIVE classes are 55 minutes.

Will this help with my menopause symptoms?

The programs focus on the five levers that matter most in peri and post menopause: sleep, protein, strategic carbs, exercise, and fueling & recovery. Many women notice better sleep, steadier energy and feeling stronger in their bodies. Everyone is different, and the programs support, but don't replace, care from your doctor.

Is it safe if I have a health condition or injury?

Most women can join safely, and every move has gentler options. If you have a health condition, an injury or are recovering from surgery, please check with your doctor first, then let Paty know so she can adapt the program for you.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

21-Day Reset: complete the 21 days, and if you don't feel stronger or more energized, email us within 7 days of finishing for a full refund. The M Program: full refund up to 7 days before your group starts; after that, you can move once to the next group. Ebooks: not refundable once downloaded. Retreats: the deposit is non-refundable; the balance is refundable up to 30 days before, then you can transfer your spot or take a credit.

Do I need equipment?

A mat, a small Pilates ball and light weights are enough to start.

Where do retreats take place?

Mostly around Vancouver. Each event page lists the location, schedule and what's included.
