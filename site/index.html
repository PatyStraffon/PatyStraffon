The Framework

How It Works

Eight weeks. One continuous practice.

Delivered through your personal coaching app — press play and move through a guided session, no exercise-by-exercise fumbling.

Weeks 1–2

Foundation

Establish the 5 Levers and build baseline habits that fit your real life.

Weeks 3–5

Build

Weeks 6–8

Integrate

Recovery, fueling, and reinforcement — so the system holds after week 8.

A Core Theme

Built with bone health in mind.

Questions

Before You Join

What if I’ve never strength-trained before?

The program meets you where you are. Sessions play through your personal coaching app at your pace, and progressions build from wherever your starting point is — there’s no prerequisite fitness level.

How much equipment do I need?

Just the basics — resistance bands, a set of light-to-moderate dumbbells, and a pilates ball cover most of the program. All of it is available in the Shop if you need to stock up.

What happens after the 8 weeks?

You can continue with M Momentum, the ongoing continuity option that keeps app access and monthly check-ins going after the core program ends.

Is this only for one stage of menopause?

The 5 Levers are designed to flex across perimenopause, menopause, and postmenopause — the framework adapts to where you are, not the other way around.

How is this different from a typical fitness program?

There’s no calorie-punishment mindset here. Exercise isn’t penance and recovery isn’t time off from training — it’s part of it. The goal is a system you can actually keep, not a 8-week sprint you white-knuckle through.
